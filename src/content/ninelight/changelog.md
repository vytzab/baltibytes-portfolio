# What's new in Ninelight

Each version of Ninelight on Google Play, newest first. Updates arrive through Google Play.

## 1.0.3 (6 November 2026)

- **Back up to Google Play Games:** keep a copy of your progress in your own Google account and get it back on a new phone, or keep two phones in step: More → Settings → Your data. It's off unless you turn it on, and free for everyone.
- **The home-screen widget shows this week's goal** under your streak.
- **On tablets and sideways,** Progress shows your skills as cards, two side by side.
- About now links to the terms of service, and the privacy policy has moved to baltibytes.com.

## 1.0.2 (28 October 2026)

- **A weekly goal** on Today, the same for everyone, Monday to Sunday: play on 4 different days, play games from 5 skills, and so on. It can be reached in the free version too.
- **18 new achievements:** 25 games of each skill, one game 25 and 100 times, 10 personal bests, the daily challenge 7 and 30 days in a row, and weekly goals reached.
- **One line about how each game went** under its score, such as "Stopped for 7 of 12 stop signs", and a new best now shows what it beat.
- **Each skill's highest score so far** on its page, and with Unlimited its change over the last 30 days.
- **Back up your progress to a file** and restore it, for example on a new phone: More → Settings → Your data. Restoring adds to what's on the phone and deletes nothing.
- **Charts for 7 days, 30 days or all time** on each game's page, and with Unlimited a sentence saying whether your scores are rising, falling or about the same.
- **Try any game for free:** every game has a free practice round, even when it isn't free today.
- **Share your daily challenge score** from the daily card, its results or the leaderboard.
- **A daily reminder, offered once after a finished workout** while it's off, at a time you choose.
- A new player's first workout starts with a game that's quick to learn.
- Large text sizes: button labels no longer break a word in two.

## 1.0.1 (27 October 2026)

- **Ninelight in Lithuanian.** Phones set to Lithuanian, or on Android 13 and later the app's own language setting, show the whole app in Lithuanian. The word games and leaderboard names stay in English.

## 1.0.0 (26 October 2026)

The first version on Google Play.

- **27 games across nine skills:** memory, attention, processing speed, logic, numeracy, spatial reasoning, language, executive function and problem solving, each with levels that follow how you play.
- **A daily workout** picked for you, a streak that forgives one rest day a week, and a home-screen widget.
- **A daily challenge** with the same puzzles for everyone, and an optional worldwide leaderboard under a made-up name.
- **Your progress:** a score for every skill, charts, personal records, insights and achievements, compared only with your own history.
- **Play together** on one phone, or challenge a friend with a code.
- **Free to play,** with a one-time Unlimited purchase for every game, every day. No subscription, no account and no ads.
