# Privacy Policy

**App:** Ninelight
**Developer:** Vytautas Zabielskas
**Contact:** baltibytes@gmail.com
**Last updated:** 2026-10-28

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: **the app has no account and collects nothing about you. Everything you play stays on your device, unless you choose to take part in the daily challenge leaderboard, which sends your daily challenge scores under a made-up name.**

## What the app stores, and where

The app keeps the following **only on your device**:

- **Your game results:** for each game you finish, its score, the levels you reached, how many answers were right, typical response times, and when you played. The app uses these to set the difficulty, pick your daily workout, and show your progress.
- **Your achievements:** which ones you have earned and when, worked out on your device from your game results.
- **Your versus matches:** for each pass-and-play match you finish, your own score and place, and the game. The other players' names and scores are only shown during the match and are never stored.
- **Challenges you have played:** which shared puzzles you have started, and your first score on each, so a replay doesn't replace it. A challenge code holds a game, its puzzles and a score, and nothing about you; the app never sends one: you share it yourself, with the app you choose.
- **Your daily challenge tries:** for each day you start the daily challenge, the game, its puzzles and level, your first try's score, and whether it went on the leaderboard.
- **Your settings:** sound and its volume, vibration, the daily reminder and its time, whether you have seen the welcome screen, and, for the leaderboard, whether you take part, your made-up name and whether others may see it.
- **Whether you have bought Unlimited,** so the app works offline.
- **How many free plays you have used today,** for the free version's daily limit.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, except what the daily challenge leaderboard sends if you choose to take part (below).

## The daily challenge leaderboard (optional)

Before your first daily challenge, the app asks whether you want to take part in a worldwide leaderboard. If you choose **Not now**, nothing is sent and the daily challenge stays on your device. You can change your answer at any time in **More → Settings → Daily challenge leaderboard**.

**If you take part**, the app sends the following to the Ninelight leaderboard server:

- **A key made on your phone.** The app creates a random cryptographic key that never leaves your phone; the server receives only its public half, which identifies you to the leaderboard. It is not linked to your Google account, your phone or your name, and every request is signed with it.
- **Your made-up name:** two words from the app's word lists and a number (for example "Quiet Heron 42"), and whether others may see it. You can't type a name in. Others see it only if you turn on **Show my name**, which is off unless you choose it.
- **For each daily challenge:** the day, that it started, and when you finish your first try, its score, the game and its rules version, whether relaxed timing was on, and the app's version.
- **A Google Play Integrity token** with each score. The server passes it to Google, which says whether the score came from the app as installed from Google Play. Google handles this under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The server keeps only whether the check passed.

**What it's used for:** ranking the day's scores and showing you your place, your percentile and how the day's scores spread, and spotting scores that can't be genuine. Nothing else: no profiling, advertising or sharing.

**Who sees what:** anyone looking at a day's leaderboard sees the names and scores of players who chose to show their name. If you don't, your score still counts towards everyone's places, but your name and row aren't shown to anyone else. Scores that look impossible are held back and checked by the developer before they appear.

**How long it's kept:** your scores and name are kept until you delete them. A game's start is kept for 7 days.

**Where it's kept:** the server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), and its database is stored in Western Europe. Like any website host, Cloudflare handles the internet connection, so it sees your IP address and technical details of each request, and may keep them in its logs for a short time to run and protect its service, under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). The leaderboard itself doesn't store your IP address.

**Your choice and your rights:** taking part is your choice, and you can stop at any time: turning **Take part in the daily leaderboard** off stops anything more being sent. **Delete my scores** in the same place removes your name and all your scores from the server for good, and the app then forgets its key. You can also ask us for a copy of what the leaderboard holds about you, or to correct or delete it, at the address below; if you no longer have the app, tell us your leaderboard name.

## What the app does not do

- No account and no sign-in, including for the leaderboard.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising, and no third-party advertising or analytics code.
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
- No sharing or selling of data.

## Purchases

The one-time Unlimited purchase is handled by **Google Play**. The app asks Google Play whether you own Unlimited and what it costs in your country. Your payment details and purchase record are handled by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The Google Play Billing library built into the app may also send Google technical information about purchase attempts, such as errors, under the same policy. We never see your payment details.

## Backup to your Google account

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include your game results, achievements, versus results and daily challenge tries in the backup it keeps in your Google account. That way your history can come back on a new phone. This is Android's own backup, stored by Google, and we cannot access it. You can turn backup off, or delete backups, in your phone's settings and your Google account.

## Backing up to a file

**More → Settings → Your data → Back up my progress** writes a file with your game results, daily workouts, achievements, versus results, daily challenge tries and settings (sound, vibration, the daily reminder and relaxed timing). It doesn't include your leaderboard key or name, your free plays or your purchase. You choose where the file goes: saved on your phone, or sent with an app you pick (for example Google Drive, email or another phone). The app itself sends it nowhere, and we never receive it unless you send it to us. Anyone you give the file to can read your results in it.

**Restore from a file** reads a file you choose and adds what's in it to the progress on your phone; it deletes nothing. The file is read only on your phone.

## Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app shows a notification on your phone at the time you choose. The reminder is created and shown on the device, with no server involved. You can turn it off in the app's settings or in Android's notification settings.

## Sound and vibration

Sounds play only on your phone, at its media volume, and can be turned off or quietened in the app's settings. The app can vibrate your phone briefly for right and wrong answers and level ups. Android grants this vibration permission when the app is installed; it gives no access to any of your data. You can turn vibration off in the app's settings, and the app also follows your phone's own touch-vibration setting.

## Information Google Play may collect

Google Play may collect information about how the app runs, for example crash reports and performance data, and share it with us in summary form through the Google Play Console. This depends on your device settings and is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## If you send feedback

More → Send feedback opens your own email app with a message to us. It is pre-filled with the app's version, your Android version and your phone model, which help with bug reports, and you can edit or delete any of it before sending. We receive only what you choose to send, and use it only to answer you and improve the app. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

## Deleting your data

Most of your data is only on your device, and you control it:

- In the app: **More → Settings → Reset all progress** deletes every result. Each game's progress screen can also delete that game's results. Achievements you have earned are kept after a reset; clearing the app's storage or uninstalling deletes them too.
- Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything the app stored.
- To delete a backup, use your phone's backup settings or your Google account. A backup file you made yourself is yours to delete wherever you saved or sent it.
- Leaderboard: **More → Settings → Daily challenge leaderboard → Delete my scores** removes your scores and name from the server. Uninstalling the app doesn't do this, as the server can't tell; delete your scores first, or email us your leaderboard name.

## Children

The app is meant for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information such as names, email addresses or contact details from anyone. The optional leaderboard uses only a made-up name and a key made on the phone.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, we will update this policy before that change reaches you, and change the date above. Online features are optional, and the app asks before anything leaves your device.
